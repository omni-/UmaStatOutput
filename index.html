What these numbers mean

Specialty EV is the card's probability of landing on its preferred training multiplied by the card's marginal rainbow output there. All-placement EV also includes its non-rainbow marginal contribution on the other four trainings, under the simplifying assumption that you follow the card and click whichever room it occupies.

Average training stats per run is career-aware. It follows Euophrys' Grand Live assumptions: 56 training turns, 20 average deck bond gained per turn, +20 global Specialty Priority by default, and a 1.4 scenario training multiplier. The five unknown deck slots are modeled with Euophrys' neutral 75-bond placeholders, so Starting Bond and support-event bond change when a card is expected to begin rainbowing.

This is still an isolated-card model rather than a full six-card simulator. Actual deck friendship multiplication, turn-selection policy, energy converting into extra actions, hints, scenario links, and context-dependent uniques can shift real results.
