What these numbers mean

Specialty Priority is the card's displayed 得意率. Specialty Rate is the calculated chance that the card appears on its preferred training. Rainbow Gain is the card's extra weighted stat contribution on a solo rainbow training compared with the same training without that card. Specialty EV combines the last two into an appearance-weighted expected contribution per turn.

The stat boxes directly below the comparison table are the selected card's per-turn Specialty EV broken out by stat. They are averages across all turns, so turns where the card does not appear are already represented.

Facility Level is a snapshot setting for uniques that explicitly scale with the current facility. The baseline gains already represent the selected late-run or summer training state, so Facility Level does not increase those base gains again. The whole-run projection instead starts facilities at Lv1 and follows the configurable Facility leveling pace.

Average training stats per run is bond-aware and scenario-aware. Grand Live uses its phase-specific unbonded/bonded values and rainbow multiplier; Unity Cup uses the corresponding Aoharu/Unity Cup training values. Starting Bond and support-event bond change when a card is expected to begin rainbowing.

This is still an isolated-card model rather than a full six-card simulator. Actual deck friendship multiplication, turn-selection policy, energy converting into extra actions, hints, scenario links, and unmodeled context-dependent uniques can shift real results.
