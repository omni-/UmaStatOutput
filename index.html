The deck projection enumerates every way the selected supports can spread across the five training rooms each turn, assumes the player takes the highest-scoring room, and totals what that produces over the run. The weighted total is absolute output including the base training the trainee would get anyway; From these supports is the part the deck is responsible for. Marginal value is what the whole deck loses if that support is dropped, and it is where placement competition finally shows up: a support that keeps landing in a room another card already owns is worth less than its solo number suggests, while two bonded supports that share their own training multiply each other's friendship bonus. Neither effect exists in the isolated per-card rows above. The Include initial stats toggle above applies here too.

Bond timing here will not match the run projection, and should not: the run projection follows one card with the deck around it unknown, while this assumes the player takes the best room every turn, which follows a good card more often and bonds it sooner.

What these numbers mean

Specialty Priority is the card's displayed 得意率. Specialty Rate is the calculated chance that the card appears on its preferred training. Rainbow Gain is the card's extra weighted stat contribution on a solo rainbow training compared with the same training without that card. Specialty EV combines the last two into an appearance-weighted expected contribution per turn.

The stat boxes directly below the comparison table are the selected card's per-turn Specialty EV broken out by stat. They are averages across all turns, so turns where the card does not appear are already represented.

Facility Level is a snapshot setting for uniques that explicitly scale with the current facility. The baseline gains already represent the selected late-run or summer training state, so Facility Level does not increase those base gains again. The whole-run projection instead starts facilities at Lv1 and follows the configurable Facility leveling pace.

Average stats per run is bond-aware and scenario-aware. It combines the card's training contribution with its guaranteed initial stats, with the initial portion shown in parentheses. Grand Live uses its phase-specific unbonded/bonded values and rainbow multiplier; Unity Cup uses the corresponding Aoharu/Unity Cup training values. Summer is a short window inside a career rather than a whole-run training state, so selecting the summer preset projects with late-run gains and the run column says so. Starting Bond and support-event bond change when a card is expected to begin rainbowing.

A ★ beside a support's name means part of its unique effect is not modeled — selecting that row spells out which part, and the number should be treated as provisional.

All-placement EV adds what the card contributes on the other four rooms to its Specialty EV. Friend and group supports have no preferred training at all, so all-placement is the only ranking that describes them, and even then it only counts their training output — their hints, energy, and event size are outside this model.

A card's marginal value is measured against the same training click without it, and that baseline keeps the trainee's own mood and growth multipliers. This deliberately differs from the upstream solo-card convention, which compares against the raw base value and so credits every card with the trainee's own mood and growth on top of its real contribution.

The per-card rows are an isolated-card model: one card, a configurable number of anonymous supports beside it, and no deck friendship multiplication. The deck projection is the six-card model — it enumerates placements, picks the best room, and prices each support by what the deck loses without it. Energy converting into extra actions, hints, race bonuses, support-event stat rewards, scenario links, and unmodeled context-dependent uniques remain outside both.
