The deck projection spreads the selected supports across the five rooms each turn, takes the highest-scoring room, and totals the run. Marginal value is what the whole deck loses if that support is dropped — placement competition and cross-card friendship bonuses show up here, not in the per-card rows above. Bond timing differs from the run view on purpose: it follows the best room each turn, so good cards bond sooner.

What these numbers mean

Specialty Rate is the chance a card appears on its own training; Rainbow Gain is its extra stat contribution on a solo rainbow; Specialty EV weights the two per turn.

All-placement EV adds what the card contributes on every other room it appears in — the only ranking that describes friend and group supports, which show — under Specialty EV and sort below every card that has one.

Facility Level is a snapshot for uniques that scale with it; the run projection starts at Lv1 instead.

Average stats per run is a bond-aware whole-run projection: training, initial stats, and once-per-run event rewards. Summer uses late-run gains (it is a window, not a whole run).

The mark beside a name grades how much of the card's unique reached the formula, not whether the card does things this tool ignores. ★ means some of it did not, so the score is missing a real term and the rank is unreliable — the ordinary effects are all modeled already, so whatever is left over is the part that makes the card worth owning, and one is enough; ☆ means everything arrived but a value is priced off an assumed context; ✓ on a FUTURE card means its unique resolved anyway, which the FUTURE chip alone cannot tell you. Selecting a row spells out which. Effects the metric never covers — hints, energy, race bonuses, failure rates — are listed there too, but separately, and never as a mark.

Per-card rows are isolated (one card, anonymous supports, no deck friendship). The deck projection is the six-card model: it enumerates placements, picks the best room, and prices each support by what the deck loses without it. Energy, hints, race bonuses, and unmodeled uniques remain outside everything.
